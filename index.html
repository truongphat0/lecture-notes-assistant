01 · Why

From pause-and-type to send-and-check

A second-year Business Administration student watches several course lectures a week (International Business, Production and Operations Management, Thinking and Decision Making…). The slow part is turning each video into notes worth revising from.

Before

  1. Watch the lecture, pause to write down timestamps
  2. Look up the business terms
  3. Type the notes into a document
  4. Write a few questions for revision
≈ 60–100 min per 15–30 min video · estimate, not timed

Now

  1. Paste the YouTube link into the Lark chat
  2. Open the document link in the reply
  3. Click 1–2 timestamps to check against the video
≈ 5 min · agent run measured at 22–38 s
02 · Workflow

What the agent does with a link

Two terminal commands from one Hermes skill, with the Profile deciding the rules.

  1. Find the YouTube linkwatch, youtu.be, shorts, live or embed; several links → one document each.
  2. Get title, channel, length and timestamped subtitlescommand 1, prepare.py; long subtitles are split into ~40,000-character chunks.
  3. Write the notes in 5 sectionsOverview · Notes by timestamp · Key terms · Takeaways for study & business · Self-test questions.
  4. Self-checktimestamps come from the subtitles, increase and stay within the video length; nothing from outside the video.
  5. Create the Lark documentcommand 2, lark_doc.py, with “anyone with the link can view”.
  6. Reply in the chatvideo title, 3–5 key points, the document link and the sharing scope.
A · no linkAsk for the link, run no command.
B · broken videoReport the exact error, never guess from the title.
C · doc failsRetry once, then send the notes in the chat.
D · special request“For a presentation” → shorter reply, still 5 sections.

05 · Limits

What it can and cannot do

Can

  • Public YouTube videos with subtitles
  • English or Vietnamese, following your message
  • Requests like “shorter” or “for a presentation”
  • Long videos, split into chunks

Cannot

  • Videos without subtitles, private or deleted videos
  • Drive, Zoom or mp4 files
  • Listening to audio, editing old documents
  • Making slides or doing homework

Needs a person

  • Clicking 1–2 timestamps before an exam or presentation
  • Approving pairing for a new user
  • Videos with poor subtitles
  • Free model limit (HTTP 429): resend later
